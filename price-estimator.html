Frequently asked questions

How much does a notary cost per signature in California?

$15 per notarized signature is the maximum a California notary may charge (Government Code §8211). That is our rate.

How are signatures counted?

Each signer's signature that is notarized counts once per document. Two people signing one deed is 2 signatures, or $30.

How much is the travel fee?

California does not set travel fees. Ours depends on distance, time of day, and urgency, and we always quote it upfront before you book. Call (858) 859-1779 for an exact total.

Who pays for a loan signing?

Loan signings are a flat rate, usually paid by the title company, escrow company, or signing service.
