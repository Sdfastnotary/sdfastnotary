Quick answer

SD Fast Notary visits seniors at home, assisted living, memory care, and skilled nursing facilities across San Diego County, 7 days a week. The signer must be alert, willing, and identifiable. The fee is $15 per signature plus a travel fee quoted upfront.

Common documents for seniors

  • Durable powers of attorney (financial)
  • Advance health care directives
  • Trust documents and certifications of trust
  • Grant deeds and trust transfer deeds
  • Bank, pension, and insurance forms

Tips for a smooth visit

  • ID: an expired ID is fine if it was issued within the last five years. Otherwise, arrange credible witnesses.
  • Timing: schedule when your loved one is usually most alert.
  • Skilled nursing facilities: an advance health care directive signed in a skilled nursing facility needs a patient advocate or ombudsman as a witness. Arrange this with the facility first.
  • Family: family members can be present, but the signer must communicate directly with the notary and sign willingly.

Frequently asked questions

Can a notary come to an assisted living facility in San Diego?

Yes. We visit assisted living, memory care, and skilled nursing facilities across San Diego County 7 days a week.

What if my parent's ID is expired?

In California an ID that is expired is still acceptable if it was issued within the last five years. Otherwise, credible witnesses can identify the signer under oath.

Can you notarize for someone with dementia?

The notary must be satisfied that the signer understands what they are signing and signs willingly. If not, the notary must decline. Consult the family's attorney about capacity questions.
