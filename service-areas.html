Frequently asked questions

Do you charge more to travel to North County or South Bay?

Travel fees depend on distance and time of day. We are based in Mira Mesa (92126), so nearby neighborhoods cost less. We always quote the total upfront. The notary fee itself is $15 per signature everywhere.

How quickly can you get to me?

Same-day appointments are often available across San Diego County. Mira Mesa, Scripps Ranch, Rancho Peñasquitos, and Carmel Valley can usually be reached fastest.

Do you serve areas outside San Diego County?

Our focus is San Diego County. For southwest Riverside County or other nearby areas, call (858) 859-1779 and we will let you know.
