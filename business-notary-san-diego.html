Quick answer

SD Fast Notary comes to your office anywhere in San Diego County to notarize contracts, corporate and HR documents, often the same day. The fee is $15 per notarized signature plus a travel fee quoted upfront, and we can handle several signers in one visit.

Documents we notarize for businesses

  • Contracts, agreements, and vendor documents
  • Corporate resolutions, meeting minutes, and bank forms
  • Affidavits and declarations for litigation and compliance

  • Vehicle and equipment titles
  • Employee documents such as travel consent letters or sworn statements

Frequently asked questions

Can you notarize documents for several employees in one visit?

Yes. Each signer needs acceptable ID and signs in front of the notary. The fee is $15 per notarized signature plus one travel fee for the visit.

Can you act as an authorized representative for Form I-9?

Employers can choose any person, including a notary, to complete Form I-9 Section 2. This is not a notarial act. Call (858) 859-1779 to check availability and fee.

Do you offer recurring appointments for offices?

Yes. Call (858) 859-1779 to set up regular visits for your office.
