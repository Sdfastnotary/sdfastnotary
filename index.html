FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How fast can a mobile notary get to me in San Diego?

Same-day appointments are often available, and many requests can be served within a few hours depending on location and schedule. Call or text (858) 859-1779 for the fastest booking.

How much does a notary cost in California?

California law sets the maximum notary fee at $15 per signature notarized. Mobile appointments add a travel fee, which we quote upfront before we confirm your appointment.

What ID do I need to bring?

Each signer needs a current, government-issued photo ID such as a driver's license, state ID card, U.S. passport, foreign passport, or U.S. military ID, current or issued within the last five years. If you don't have acceptable ID, ask us about using credible witnesses.

Should I sign my document before the notary arrives?

No. For most documents, such as acknowledgments and jurats, you must sign in front of the notary. Leave the signature lines blank until your appointment.

Can a notary give legal advice or prepare my documents?

No. A California notary public who is not an attorney cannot give legal advice or prepare legal documents. We verify identity and witness signatures; please consult an attorney for legal questions.

Do you notarize at hospitals, care homes and jails?

Yes. We regularly visit hospitals, assisted living facilities, and detention facilities across San Diego County. Signers must be alert, willing, and have acceptable ID.
