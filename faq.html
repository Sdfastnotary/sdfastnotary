Preparing for your appointment

What ID do I need for a notary in California?

A current government-issued photo ID, or one issued within the last five years, such as a California driver's license, U.S. passport, military ID, or another state's license. Full ID guide.

Should I sign before the notary arrives?

No. Leave your document unsigned. For acknowledgments and jurats you must sign, or acknowledge your signature, in front of the notary.

Can you notarize a document with blank spaces?

A notary should not notarize an incomplete document. Fill in blanks or mark them N/A before the appointment.

Do all signers need to be present?

Every person whose signature will be notarized must appear in person in front of the notary with acceptable ID.

Why do you need my thumbprint?

California law requires a thumbprint in the notary journal for deeds affecting real property and for powers of attorney. It helps prevent fraud.

What a notary can and cannot do

Can a notary give legal advice?

No. A California notary who is not an attorney cannot give legal advice, choose your form, or explain the legal effect of a document.

Can a California notary certify a copy of a birth certificate or passport?

No. California notaries can only certify copies of powers of attorney and their own journal entries. Request certified vital records from the issuing agency.

Can you notarize a document in Spanish or another language?

Yes, if the notary can communicate directly with the signer. The notarial certificate is completed in English. California does not allow notarizing through a third-party interpreter.

Can a notary notarize a will in California?

California wills generally need two witnesses rather than notarization. A notary can still notarize related estate documents, such as a certification of trust or a deed transferring property into a trust.

Can a notary refuse to notarize?

Yes. A notary must refuse if the signer cannot be properly identified, appears not to understand the document, or seems to be signing under pressure.
