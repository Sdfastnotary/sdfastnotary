Notary service that comes to you

Skip the bank line and the UPS store. SD Fast Notary travels to your location anywhere in San Diego County, including homes, offices, hospitals, assisted living facilities, coffee shops, and detention centers. Appointments are available early mornings, evenings, and weekends.

Documents we commonly notarize

  • Powers of attorney (general, durable, healthcare)
  • Advance healthcare directives
  • Grant deeds, quitclaim deeds, and trust transfer deeds
  • Affidavits, declarations, and sworn statements (jurats)
  • Travel consent letters for minors
  • Vehicle and DMV forms, bills of sale
  • Business contracts and corporate documents
  • Estate planning signings (trusts, certifications of trust)

What to have ready

  • A current government-issued photo ID for every signer
  • Your complete, unsigned document (sign in front of the notary)
  • All signers present, alert, and willing to sign

Pricing

California law caps notary fees at $15 per notarized signature. Mobile service adds a travel fee based on distance and time of day, and we quote it upfront before we confirm your appointment.

Wills are not typically notarized in California, and a notary cannot advise which form you need. Please consult an attorney for legal advice.
